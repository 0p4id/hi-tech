Certified Master Technicians

Hi-Tech Automotive only employs Certified Master Technicians. We take quality and customer service seriously and follow a strict code of ethics, so customers know upfront what to expect. As part of that code, we pledge to perform high quality repair services.

Owner Fred A. has been working on cars since 1988 and started his first location in San Bernardino. He graduated in automotive electronic engineering and holds several automotive technology certificates.
